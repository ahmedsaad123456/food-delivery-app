### Inventory Concurrency & Overselling Protection

- Added a concurrency test using `ExecutorService` and `CountDownLatch` to simulate 50 threads attempting to purchase the last 5 items, reproducing the overselling problem.
- Implemented and tested three concurrency control strategies:
  - **Optimistic Locking:** Added `@Version` with retry handling for `OptimisticLockingFailureException`.
  - **Pessimistic Locking:** Added `@Lock(LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE)` to the repository query.
  - **Atomic SQL Update:** Used a conditional `UPDATE` statement to prevent stock from dropping below zero.
- Compared all three approaches in terms of simplicity and performance under high concurrency.
- Selected the most suitable approach for inventory management.
